# The Bored Room

**Project Type:** Branding / Identity, Illustration, Print

The Bored Room was a room design project for QCA, created in response to the lack of social culture and limited social opportunities on campus. The goal was to design a space where students could relax, connect, and exchange creative ideas.

The full design scope included logo design, flyers, posters, and 3D spatial design. The creative concept was inspired by an abstract beach, using beach and sofa-inspired colors to create a relaxing and comfortable atmosphere for students after class.

The design also used playful line elements to contrast with the idea of a "boring room," reflecting the diversity, creativity, and social energy of the space.
