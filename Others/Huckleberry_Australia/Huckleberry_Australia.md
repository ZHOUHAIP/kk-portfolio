# Huckleberry Australia

**Project Type:** Branding / Identity, Illustration, Print

Huckleberry Australia is a Japanese intermediary company that provides education and travel guidance in Australia for international students.

The full design scope included the logo, business cards, letterhead, envelopes, T-shirts, and other brand applications.

Orange was selected as the main brand color to represent the company's passion and enthusiastic service. The logo combines a circular form with the letter **H**, reflecting the company's professionalism, friendliness, and commitment to supporting international students from around the world.
