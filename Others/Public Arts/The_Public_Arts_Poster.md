# The Public Arts Poster

**Project Type:** Creative Poster Design

This poster was designed to introduce an app that helps users discover public art around them and explore it for free. The poster communicates the app's core concept, target audience, and key functional steps.

The visual concept was inspired by the bright contrasting colors often found in public art, using yellow, pink, and green as the main palette. Combined with geometric background elements, the design creates a bold and dynamic visual language that highlights the theme of public art.
