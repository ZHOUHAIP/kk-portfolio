# Quote "Excellence"

**Project Type:** Creative, Illustration, Print

This project was a poster design inspired by the quote: **"Excellence is a habit, not an act. It takes practice and perseverance."**

The full design scope included posters, screensavers, phone cases, T-shirts, computer cases, and other applications.

The creative concept was based on the idea of climbing. Reaching the top of a mountain represents the pursuit of excellence, while the journey upward symbolizes the practice, perseverance, and continuous effort required to achieve it.
