# 2023 EC Holiday Campaign

The digital activation concept was built around **"The Snow Globe"**, creating an interactive snowfall experience that engaged customers and evoked a sense of love and romance.

With each phone shake, users triggered snowfall and discovered a new product with tailored copy. This playful interaction enhanced user engagement and helped drive sales conversion.

For this project, I supported the production of all visual and motion assets and worked closely with third-party developers to complete and launch the final digital experience. The campaign received strong feedback and delivered solid performance results.
