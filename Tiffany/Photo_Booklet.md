# NSO Bespoke Photo Frame

*Collaboration with Illustrator and Events Team*

For Tiffany's new store openings in Shenzhen and Beijing, we developed bespoke photo frame designs tailored to each city, highlighting the unique store identity and strengthening audience engagement.

For Shenzhen, the design was inspired by the brand's **"Love for Our Oceans"** theme, under the concept **"From the Tiffany window, one can see the world of Shenzhen."**

For Beijing, the design centered on the **Chinese New Year** theme, under the concept **"Through a Chinese window, one can experience the world of Tiffany."**

I collaborated with a third-party illustrator throughout the project, providing the creative brief, managing the timeline, and coordinating multiple rounds of design reviews to ensure the final artwork met project objectives. I also worked closely with the Events team to support production and ensure the final deliverables were successfully implemented.
