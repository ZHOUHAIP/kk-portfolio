# EuroEyes

**Project Type:** Marketing / Branding, Medical

EuroEyes is an international eye medical center dedicated to providing comprehensive eye care solutions. As a global leader in eye laser procedures, including SMILE and LASIK, as well as lens treatments such as multifocal lenses, ICL, and cataract surgery, EuroEyes helps patients reduce or eliminate their dependence on glasses and contact lenses.

The full design scope included brand videos, opening key visual boards, LED clinic screens, website design, flyers, posters, and online advertising materials.
