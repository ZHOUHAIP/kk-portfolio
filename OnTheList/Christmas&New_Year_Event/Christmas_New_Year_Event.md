# Christmas & New Year Event

**Role:** UI Designer, Visual Designer  
**Timeline:** 1 month  
**Tools:** Sketch, Illustrator, Photoshop

## Project Overview

As the Christmas and New Year season approached, more customers were expected to shop through the Mini Program to choose gifts for friends and family. The company aimed to enhance the online shopping experience by creating a festive visual atmosphere across the Mini Program.

## Goals

To provide users with a smooth and engaging shopping experience through Christmas and New Year themed visual design within the online Mini Program.
