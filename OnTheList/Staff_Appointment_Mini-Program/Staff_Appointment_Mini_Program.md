# Staff Appointment Mini Program

**Role:** UI/UX Designer  
**Client/Product:** OnTheList  
**Deliverables:** User flows, wireframes, high-fidelity UI design, clickable prototypes

## Project Overview

The Staff Appointment Mini Program was developed by OnTheList for brand partners, allowing brand staff to book appointments for offline events and access online shopping events with discounted pricing.

## Project Goals

- Enable brand staff to book offline event appointments smoothly
- Provide a seamless online shopping experience with exclusive discounted prices

## Role

As the UI/UX Designer on the team, I developed high-fidelity wireframes, clickable prototypes, and user flows for the employee booking Mini Program to improve user engagement and strengthen brand-business collaboration. I worked closely with product managers and developers to ensure the appointment Mini Program ran smoothly.

## Problem

The existing Staff Appointment Mini Program had an overly simplistic interface, which weakened the overall brand image. Brand partners wanted to further develop the Mini Program with a more premium visual experience to better build trust with users.

## Process

To better understand users' pain points when making appointments through the Mini Program, we conducted several user interviews, collected 30 user surveys, and carried out usability testing on the existing Mini Program. We gathered the findings and synthesized key problems and potential solutions through affinity mapping and user journey analysis.

## User Research

Our user research showed that users preferred clear information, simple steps, and user-friendly features during the booking process.

- Pictures are better than words
- A phrase is better than a sentence
- Visual icons are better than text

## Competitive Analysis

- Considerable Title.
- Description/subtitle: explain what's the title meaningful?
- Call to action: offer two options for user to choose
- Brand visual image / logo on top
- Clean and clear layout, brand identity color
