# Offline Design

**Project Type:** Marketing / Advertising, Illustration, Print

This project covered a wide range of offline creative and marketing design materials for both internal events and the EF Teacher Recruitment Marketing Team.

The internal creative design materials included custom fans, table boards, and eco bags for the summer party.

For the EF Teacher Recruitment Marketing Team, the design scope included social media cards, brochures, referral posters, TRT offsite T-shirts for Macau, prospect magazine pages, bookmarks, competition posters, and A6 postcards.

The goal was to create visually engaging and brand-consistent materials that supported internal engagement, recruitment communication, and offline marketing activities.
