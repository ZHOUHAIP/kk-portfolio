# Online Design

**Project Type:** Marketing / Advertising, Online, Web

This project covered a range of online marketing and digital design assets across social media, web, and owned digital channels.

The design scope included social media ads for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and WeChat, covering infographic designs, quote visuals, and short video content. It also included web banner designs for recruitment websites, WeChat Official Account images, and landing page designs for the company website.

The goal was to create consistent, engaging, and platform-appropriate visual assets to support online marketing communication and campaign performance.
